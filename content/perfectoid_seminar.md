# Perfectoid and prismatic methods

The seminar takes place on mondays in room MA-B1-524 from 10:15 to 12:00. A discussion/exercise session takes places in the same room from 13:15 to 14:00.

I will give at least the first two sessions. Then participants are invited to present topics. Topics are listed below. There will be a priori 14 spot for talks. Talks below may be split in two or skipped if needed.

**Seminar schedule**

1. _(7 september: Léo)_ Introduction to perfectoid and prismatic methods. (By Zoom!) No exercice session. [Notes.](/pdf/ppmLec1.pdf)

2. _(14 september: Léo)_ Perfectoid rings, perfect prisms, Tilting equivalance. [Notes.](/pdf/ppmLec2.pdf)
3. _(21 september: Léo)_ Perfectoidization, arc topology, André's Lemma, Almost purity.
4. _(28 september)_  Perfectizations. (Aspects of p-adic Hodge theory. Section 4)
5. _(5 ocotber)_ Perfectoid crystals and overconvergent sheaves. (Aspects of p-adic Hodge theory. Section 5)
6. _(12 october)_ Riemman-Hilbert. (Aspects of p-adic Hodge theory. Section 6)
7. _(26 october)_ Almost coherence and RH (Aspects of p-adic Hodge theory. Section 7)
8. _(2 november)_ Duality and t-exactness properties (Aspects of p-adic Hodge theory. Section 9 and 10)
9. _(9 november)_ Applications (Aspects of p-adic Hodge theory. Section 8 and 11).
10. _(16 november: Léo)_ Bhatt Cohen-Macaullayness and applications. [arXiv:2008.08070v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.08070)
11. _(23 november)_ Applcations to MMP [arXiv:2012.15801v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.15801)
12. _(30 november)_ Survey of prismatic cohomology.
